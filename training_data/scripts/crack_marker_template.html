Crack Marker

How to use

  • Scroll over the waveform to zoom around the mouse; drag to pan. Click the top strip to jump anywhere.
  • Play view plays just what's on screen (slowed, looping) so you can listen to a few seconds at a time.
  • Double-click on a crack to mark it. Or press M while it plays to mark at the playhead (then fine-tune by double-clicking the exact spike).
  • Cracks look like thin bright vertical lines in the lower picture (steady noise is dimmed out) and sharp spikes in the waveform.
  • If something you marked is NOT a crack (bean rattle, a knock), mark it with N / shift-double-click instead — "not a crack" examples help too.
  • Space play/stop · +/− zoom · Z undo last mark.
  • When done, press Copy marks and paste them into the chat.
